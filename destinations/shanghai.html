Top Experiences

What defines Shanghai

The Bund

Best at sunset and after dark. Walk slowly and allow time for skyline photography.

1–2 hoursEasy

Yu Garden

Arrive early for traditional pavilions, ponds, rockeries, and old-city lanes.

1.5–2 hoursEasy

French Concession

Tree-lined streets, cafés, villas, and a relaxed afternoon pace.

2–4 hoursEasy

Pudong

Modern architecture and elevated city views across the river.

Autumn Note

Seasonal character

Autumn is subtle in central Shanghai, but cooler air and warm late-afternoon light make city walks more comfortable.
