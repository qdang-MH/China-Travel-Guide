Travel Resources

Payments, rail, packing, language, and emergencies

Payments

Set up Alipay and WeChat Pay before departure. Carry a backup card and some RMB.

Connectivity

Arrange roaming or an eSIM. Save hotel names and addresses offline.

High-Speed Rail

Arrive 45–60 minutes early, keep passports ready, and monitor the departure gate.

Private Transfers

Confirm driver name, phone number, exact pickup point, and hotel in Chinese.

Emergency Numbers

Police 110 · Ambulance 120 · Fire 119.

Useful Phrase

请带我们去这家酒店。
Please take us to this hotel.
