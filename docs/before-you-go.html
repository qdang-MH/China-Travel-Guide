Before You Go

Documents, apps, money, internet, and packing

Documents

Prepare these first

Passport

Confirm validity and keep a digital backup stored securely.

Visa and Entry Rules

Check requirements for every traveler close to departure.

Insurance

Carry policy numbers and emergency contact details offline.

Apps

Install before departure

Alipay

Primary mobile-payment option for many travelers.

WeChat

Messaging, payments, drivers, and hotel contacts.

Amap

Best local map and navigation tool inside mainland China.

Trip.com

Rail, hotel, and transport confirmations.

Google Translate

Download Chinese for offline use.

Pleco

Useful Chinese dictionary and character lookup.

Money & Connectivity

Keep backup options

Payments

  • Set up mobile payments early
  • Carry one backup card
  • Keep some RMB cash
  • Tell the bank about travel

Internet

  • Arrange roaming or eSIM
  • Save hotel addresses offline
  • Download maps before mountain days
  • Carry a power bank
Packing

Core packing list

City Days

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Light layers
  • Compact umbrella
  • Small day bag

Huangshan

  • Warm layers
  • Gloves
  • Rain shell
  • Small overnight bag
  • Medication and blister care
