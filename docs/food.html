Ordering Strategy

Make shared meals easy

Control Spice

Ask for mild dishes and order one spicy specialty rather than making every dish hot.

Share Widely

For five adults, order several dishes in the center so everyone can sample.

Keep a Backup

On long mountain days, carry snacks and do not rely solely on scenic-area restaurants.
