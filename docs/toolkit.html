Prepare

Travel Toolkit

Payments, connectivity, packing, emergency basics, and useful Chinese.

Payments

Set up Alipay and WeChat Pay before departure. Carry a backup card and a modest RMB reserve.

Connectivity

Arrange roaming or an eSIM. Save hotel and driver information offline.

Rail

Arrive early, keep passports accessible, and monitor the departure gate.

Health

Carry regular medication, blister care, motion-sickness medicine, and basic first aid.

Mountain Bag

Use a small overnight bag for Huangshan; leave large luggage below.

Emergency

Police: 110 · Ambulance: 120 · Fire: 119. Verify local assistance with the hotel.
