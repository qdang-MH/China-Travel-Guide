Station Guide

Recognize the major transport points

Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station

Departure point for the high-speed rail journey toward Huangshan.

  • Arrive 45–60 minutes early
  • Keep passports accessible
  • Allow time for security screening
  • Check the gate on the departure board
  • Buy snacks before boarding
Open photo/source details →

Huangshan North Railway Station

Main high-speed rail gateway for the Huangshan region.

  • Arrange the private driver in advance
  • Confirm the meeting point
  • Allow for luggage loading
  • Keep the hotel name in Chinese
  • Expect a road transfer after arrival
Open photo/source details →

Zhangjiajie Railway Station

A useful reference station for Zhangjiajie transfers; final station depends on the booked route.

  • Verify whether the ticket uses Zhangjiajie or Zhangjiajie West
  • Share the exact station name with the driver
  • Do not assume both stations are interchangeable
  • Allow a generous pickup buffer
Open photo/source details →

Fenghuang Gucheng Railway Station

High-speed rail access for Fenghuang Ancient Town.

  • The station is outside the ancient town
  • Arrange onward transport before arrival
  • Confirm the hotel meeting point
  • Keep small luggage accessible
Open photo/source details →

Chengdu East Railway Station

Major Chengdu high-speed rail hub with metro and taxi connections.

  • Follow signs carefully in the large concourse
  • Use the official taxi queue
  • Keep the hotel address offline
  • Allow extra walking time inside the station
Open photo/source details →
