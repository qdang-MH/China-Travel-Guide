Trip 1 · Day 2

Old Shanghai and the Bund

Shanghai — A classic first full day combining gardens, local food, neighborhoods, and skyline views.

TransportTaxi + walking
Walking8–11 km
WeatherCool and dry
Budget$65–$140

Today’s Plan

Follow the day in sequence

8:00 AM

Breakfast

Eat a full breakfast before sightseeing.

9:30 AM

Yu Garden

Visit before the busiest period.

12:00 PM

Shanghai lunch

Try xiaolongbao, noodles, vegetables, and mild shared dishes.

2:00 PM

French Concession

Walk leafy streets, cafés, shops, and restored neighborhoods.

6:00 PM

Bund evening

Return for blue-hour and night skyline views.

Traveler Notes

  • Hotel: Waldorf Astoria Shanghai on the Bund
  • Carry: Comfortable shoes, power bank, light jacket, tissues
  • Food: Xiaolongbao, scallion-oil noodles, shengjian bao
  • Best advice: Schedule the Bund twice—daylight and after dark feel completely different.
