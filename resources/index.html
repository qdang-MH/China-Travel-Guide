Before You Go

Travel Resources

Payments, rail, connectivity, packing, and emergencies

Payments

Set up Alipay and WeChat Pay before departure. Carry a backup card and some RMB.

Connectivity

Arrange roaming or an eSIM. Save hotel names and addresses offline.

High-Speed Rail

Arrive 45–60 minutes early and keep passports accessible.

Private Transfers

Confirm driver name, plate, phone number, and pickup point.

Huangshan Packing

Warm layers, gloves, rain shell, water, medication, and a small overnight bag.

Emergency Numbers

Police 110 · Ambulance 120 · Fire 119.
