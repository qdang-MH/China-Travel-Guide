Days 1–3

Shanghai

Modern China, classic neighborhoods, and a gentle first landing

Begin with three unhurried nights near the Bund. The plan mixes old Shanghai, modern skyline views, excellent food, and enough flexibility to recover from the international flight.
Days 4–5

Hangzhou

West Lake, tea culture, temples, and golden autumn color

Hangzhou is the romantic center of the trip. West Lake, Lingyin Temple, and Longjing tea country create a slower, softer chapter after Shanghai.
Day 6

Wuzhen

Canals, stone bridges, lanterns, and an overnight water-town experience

Wuzhen is deliberately an overnight stop. The key experiences happen after day visitors leave: lantern-lit canals, reflections, quiet lanes, and a calmer morning.
Days 7–9

Huangshan

Granite peaks, sunrise, clouds, and late-autumn mountain scenery

Huangshan is the scenic centerpiece. The route protects time for weather changes, a cable-car ascent, a mountain overnight, sunset, and an optional sunrise.
Day 9 excursion

Hongcun

Reflection ponds, white walls, black roofs, and slow village exploration

Hongcun is the human-scale cultural counterpoint to Huangshan. The visit is intentionally slow, centered on ponds, lanes, architecture, tea, and photography.
