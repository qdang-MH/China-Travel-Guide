Trip 1 Toolkit

Travel Prepared

Practical guidance for money, connectivity, rail, luggage, health, and group review.

Before Departure

Core setup

Payments

Set up Alipay and WeChat Pay before departure. Carry a backup card and small RMB cash reserve.

Connectivity

Arrange roaming or an eSIM, test important apps, and save hotel addresses offline.

Documents

Carry passport, insurance details, reservations, and digital copies.

Rail

Arrive early, keep passports accessible, and reserve seats together when sales open.

Mountain Bag

Use one small overnight bag for Huangshan and leave large luggage below.

Health

Bring regular medication, motion-sickness medicine, blister care, and a compact first-aid kit.

Packing

November essentials

Layers

Base layer, fleece, light insulated layer, rain shell.

Footwear

Walking shoes with grip, extra socks, optional hiking shoes.

Mountain Items

Gloves, warm hat, water bottle, small backpack.

Daily Carry

Passport, phone, power bank, tissues, sanitizer, medication.

Review Checklist

Questions for the group

Pacing

Does any day feel too long or too active?

Hotels

Are the selected comfort level and estimated prices acceptable?

Walking

Does anyone need easier alternatives on Days 6, 8, 9, or 10?

Food

Are there allergies, spice limits, or familiar-meal requirements?

Transfers

Are the long travel days acceptable with no evening plans?

Final Changes

Record agreed changes before booking transportation and hotels.
